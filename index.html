2. Here's the plan to win it back

Strategic brief · 90-day plan · 180-day plan
Strategic Brief

Hex is losing its own category on the searches buyers use most.

When buyers compare notebook tools, they ask Google and ChatGPT first. Deepnote already owns those answers. They've built 30+ comparison pages and rank #1 for "hex vs deepnote," "hex vs jupyter," and "hex vs colab."

We tested 7 obvious Hex comparison URLs — /compare/hex-vs-deepnote, /compare/hex-vs-mode, /compare/hex-vs-databricks, and four others. Every one returned a 404. Only two comparison pages exist today (/compare/sigma-vs-hex and /compare/looker-vs-hex), and both rank well when they're live. The pages buyers are searching for simply don't exist.

The structural picture makes it worse. Across our 16-page sample of hex.tech, 0% have a TL;DR summary at the top — the block AI engines copy into their answers. 25% have FAQ-style content, but 0% use the markup AI engines need to read it. When AI pulls from a Hex page, it rarely names Hex.

Offsite is the biggest gap. Across 15,157 third-party citations AI engines read for this category, Hex shows up in 0%. Reddit (2,792 citations), YouTube (1,204), Wikipedia (1,009), and Medium (983) drive the answers, and Hex isn't named in any of them.

For category searches like "data visualization tool" (2,900/mo), "ai agent for data analysis," and "snowflake notebook," Deepnote, Julius, and Databricks own the AI answer. Hex doesn't appear.

The bright spot: when Hex IS named in AI answers ("hex vs deepnote," "hex alternatives," "agentic analytics"), the citation leans on third-party sources. The brand voice works when it shows up — there just isn't enough of it out there.

A Top Recommendations
  1. 01

    Win the AI Overview for "best data notebook" — offsite.

    Problem When buyers ask ChatGPT or Google's AI Overview about the category, Deepnote, Databricks, and Splunk get named — not Hex. 85% of AI brand mentions come from third-party sources Hex isn't in.
    Fix A 3-part offsite push: (1) commission a Wikipedia entry for Hex Technologies (every named competitor has one), (2) get Hex into the editorial roundups currently winning that query, (3) distribute the State of Data Teams 2026 stats to Datanami, The New Stack, InfoWorld, and Forrester.
    Why it matters Single highest-ROI move in the audit. Brands publishing original research get cited 3–7× more once it's distributed.
  2. 02

    Ship the buyer pages that don't exist yet.

    Problem Buyers compare notebook tools on Google before they pick. Deepnote ranks #1 for "hex vs deepnote," "hex vs jupyter," and "hex vs colab." Hex's matching URLs return 404.
    Fix AirOps ships 10–12 new /compare/ pages (hex-vs-deepnote, hex-vs-mode, hex-vs-databricks, and seven others) using the existing /compare/sigma-vs-hex/ template.
    Why it matters Today, every buyer searching "Hex vs Deepnote" reads Deepnote's version. Closing the page set wins the comparison search Hex already deserves.
  3. 03

    Clean up the site so AI can actually read it.

    Problem Hex has 377 verified G2 reviews at 4.7 stars and FAQ-shaped content across the blog — but AI engines can't extract any of it because the page markup isn't there. A repeated nav banner also injects 100–150 words of duplicate copy into the top of every page.
    Fix Deploy machine-readable Q&A and review markup across all 9 product pages and every /compare/ page, plus a one-line engineering fix to render the nav banner once instead of six times.
    Why it matters Two pattern-based fixes make every existing page AI-citable — no new writing required.
